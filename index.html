GLP-1 companion

Beginner-friendly tracking for people using pens or reconstituted vials. The app keeps reconstitution and additional compounds available, but the main experience stays simple.

  • Track injections, site rotation, weight and side effects.
  • Log food tolerance and possible symptom patterns.
  • Monitor costs, remaining vial amount and refill timing.
  • Export data for your own records or to discuss with a clinician.

Red flags

Seek urgent medical advice for severe abdominal pain, repeated vomiting, dehydration, chest pain, breathlessness, severe allergic reaction, fainting, confusion, or injection-site infection.

Storage reminders

Record the label storage instructions, mix date, water amount, storage location, expiry/discard date and any visual changes. Avoid heat, sunlight and contamination risk.
